Barn Etiquette & Tips

Never Line Danced Before? Here's the Code:

You don't need fancy boots or western fringe to join in. Line dancing is about community, joy, and moving together in unison.

directions_walk Center Floor is for Beginners

Stand in the middle row so you always have someone in front of you to watch when we rotate walls!

roller_skating Smooth-Soled Footwear

Leather-soled boots or flat street shoes work best so your knees turn freely without sticking to the maple boards.

sentiment_very_satisfied Mistakes Are Just Variations

If you miss a step, just laugh, step in place, and jump right back in on the next 8-count.

group Partner Not Required

Over 70% of our dancers show up solo. By the second song, you'll know everyone in your row.
